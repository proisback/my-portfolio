# Brag Plan: Prateek Mehta, the flight portfolio

## What is this app?
A personal PM portfolio that is a scroll-driven 3D flight over Prateek's real career cities (Mumbai → Jamshedpur → Chennai → Mumbai), drawn in blueprint ink that fills with color as it reaches 2026, ending on a split-flap departures board of his 7 AI products.

## The angle
A career told as a flight. The video plays like an in-flight film: real footage of the site's 3D world, with airport-style typography (IATA codes, a year odometer, a split-flap board). The specific magic is the blueprint-to-color transformation: ops years drawn in ink, the 2026 "build" years painted in by a marigold wave over night Mumbai. Every frame is the real site, captured at exact flight states, not a mock-up.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Blueprint Mumbai drawn in ink, the plane on the runway, then rolling into take-off. Line: "I turned my career into a flight."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The route: a cloud punch-through, Jamshedpur's steel plant smoke, the Chennai coast, with a BOM → IXW → MAA → BOM banner and the year rolling 2012 → 2025.
- The cockpit: the camera settles into the captain's seat and the marigold AI sidekick waves from the co-pilot seat, then the lit night skyline through the windscreen.
- The payoff: the marigold wave sweeps night Mumbai from blueprint into full color while the site's thesis lands: "Stop fixing systems. Start building them."
- The departures board: split-flap rows clatter in, "7 AI products built in 2026".

## Outro / punchline
End card in the site's boarding-pass style: "Prateek Mehta · Product Manager who builds with AI", the URL, and "Designed and built with Claude Code."

## User flow worth showing
Scroll is the product: hero → take-off → cities → cockpit → departures board → boarding pass opens a case study. The video follows that order, compressed.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: in-flight film for a career; calm, premium, airline-precise
- Interpretation: fewer scenes with confident holds, big but restrained type, soft scaled crossfades; no hype words, the footage carries the wow.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 24s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #F5EFE1 (paper), night #12172A
- Accent: #F5C94C (marigold), deep #C98A12
- Text: #1F1A1D (ink), #F4EEDD on night
- Blueprint ink: #22304A
- Display font: Sora 700 (tight tracking)
- Body / metadata font: IBM Plex Mono 400/500 (uppercase, tracked)
- Strongest visual element: the blueprint-to-color wave over night Mumbai; the split-flap departures board

## Share copy (draft)
I rebuilt my portfolio as a flight: eight years across Mumbai, Jamshedpur and Chennai, drawn in blueprint ink that fills with color as it lands in 2026. No 3D models, every building and my AI co-pilot are code.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady, clean)
- Music treatment: start at 0, bed around 0.32, fade in over 0.6s, fade out over the last 1.2s
- Music cue guidance: preset read (110 BPM). Strong cues: 8.74s (cut to cockpit), 13.11s (cut to the wave), 17.47s (board reveal), 22.93s (URL lands). Beat grid every ~0.545s for the board's row-by-row clatter.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS warms the marigold glow on the thesis and end-card stub. No visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, polished
- Audio-coupled moments: cloud punch whoosh-like soft impact, a soft click when the board rows land (first and last only), a single bell when the URL lands.
- Restraint rule: no SFX over readable lines except the board; never louder than the bed's peaks.

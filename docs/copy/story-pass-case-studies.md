**Word count: 2,668 → ~1,300 (about −51%)** across the 7 pages. Every fact below already appears on that page or in your resume. Tags work as before: **[S]** sourced, **[F]** new framing you need to confirm. Rows marked **"(unchanged)"** stay word for word.

## The pattern, same on every page

1. **A 3-line opener** replaces the tagline and summary under the boarding pass. Styled like a flight manifest: `PROBLEM` / `WHAT I DID` / `RESULT`, each one sentence, and the result carries a number already on the page. A recruiter who reads only these three lines still gets the case.
2. **Section headers become hooks.** "The Problem" becomes the problem's sharpest line.
3. **Paragraphs of 3 sentences or fewer.** Numbers move out of prose and into the stat tiles the pages already have.
4. **"What This Demonstrates": cut on all 7.** The opener and the 3 tags at the top already say what each case shows. Where a section holds a fact found nowhere else, that fact moves up into the page (noted per page).

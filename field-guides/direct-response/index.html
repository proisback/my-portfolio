Pinned Sources & Attribution

  • Eugene Schwartz, "Breakthrough Advertising" (1966) — the five stages of awareness, the five stages of market sophistication, mass desire, mechanism escalation.
  • Claude Hopkins, "Scientific Advertising" (1923, public domain) — salesmanship in print, reason-why, specificity, testing, the unique-mechanism root (Schlitz).
  • Robert Cialdini, "Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion" (New & Expanded, 2021) — social proof, authority, scarcity, reciprocity.
  • John Caples, "Tested Advertising Methods" (5th ed., 1998) — judge by response, specificity, objection handling. Earlier editions retain more of Caples' original.
  • David Ogilvy, "Ogilvy on Advertising" (1983) — the big idea, clarity over cleverness.
  • Modern practitioners: Gary Bencivenga (proof beats persuasion, believability); Todd Brown (the unique mechanism); Jay Abraham (risk reversal); Alex Hormozi, "$100M Offers" (2021, the value equation).
